Formula 1, also called F1, is a popular car racing sport that happens around the world. The cars are very fast and are designed with advanced technology. Many people watch F1 because the races are exciting and full of action.

In F1, drivers compete in different countries during a season to earn points and win the championship. Each team works hard to improve their cars and strategy for every race. I like F1 because it shows speed, teamwork, and skill at the highest level.
