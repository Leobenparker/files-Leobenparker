Club Career

FC Barcelona (2004–2021)

Lionel Messi made his first-team debut for FC Barcelona in 2004. During his time at the club, he became Barcelona's all-time leading scorer and helped the team win numerous trophies. Messi formed legendary partnerships with players such as Xavi, Andrés Iniesta, and Neymar.

At Barcelona, Messi won multiple La Liga titles, UEFA Champions League trophies, Copa del Rey titles, and individual awards. His incredible performances made him one of the greatest players in football history.

Paris Saint-Germain (2021–2023)

In 2021, Messi joined Paris Saint-Germain after leaving Barcelona. While playing in France, he helped PSG win league titles and continued to demonstrate his outstanding passing, creativity, and goal-scoring ability.

Inter Miami (2023–Present)

Messi joined Inter Miami in Major League Soccer in 2023. His arrival attracted worldwide attention and significantly increased interest in football across the United States. He quickly became one of the most important players on the team.

Major Club Achievements

  • 10 La Liga Titles
  • 4 UEFA Champions League Titles
  • 7 Copa del Rey Titles
  • Multiple Spanish Super Cups
  • Ligue 1 Champion with PSG
  • Leagues Cup Champion with Inter Miami
