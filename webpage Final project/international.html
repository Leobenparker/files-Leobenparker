International Career

Argentina National Team

Lionel Messi made his debut for the Argentina National Team in 2005. Over the years, he became the captain and one of the most important players in the history of Argentine football. His leadership and talent helped Argentina compete at the highest level of international football.

Copa America Success

After several difficult tournament finals, Messi finally achieved international success in 2021 when Argentina defeated Brazil in the Copa America Final. This victory was one of the most emotional moments of his career and ended a long wait for a major international trophy.

2022 FIFA World Cup

The greatest achievement of Messi's international career came in 2022. Argentina won the FIFA World Cup in Qatar after defeating France in one of the most exciting finals in football history. Messi played a crucial role throughout the tournament and was awarded the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player.

International Records

  • Argentina's all-time leading goal scorer

Legacy with Argentina

Messi's success with Argentina completed one of the greatest football careers ever. Winning both the Copa America and FIFA World Cup strengthened his place among the greatest athletes in sports history.
