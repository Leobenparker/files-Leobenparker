Early Life

Lionel Andrés Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina. From a young age, he showed exceptional football talent and spent much of his childhood playing the game with friends and family.

Family Background

Messi grew up in a supportive family. His father, Jorge Messi, worked in a steel factory, while his mother, Celia Cuccittini, worked in manufacturing. His family played an important role in encouraging his football dreams.

Health Challenges

At the age of 11, Messi was diagnosed with a growth hormone deficiency. The treatment was expensive, making it difficult for his family to afford. Despite these challenges, Messi continued to pursue his passion for football.

Newell's Old Boys

Messi joined the youth academy of Newell's Old Boys, where he quickly became one of the most talented young players in Argentina. His performances caught the attention of football scouts from around the world.

Move to Barcelona

At age 13, Messi moved to Spain after FC Barcelona offered to support his medical treatment and invited him to join their famous youth academy, La Masia. This decision changed the course of his life and career.
