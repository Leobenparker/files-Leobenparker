Why I Like these Scriptures

A student once faced many challenges at once—he was nervous about leading a project, stressed about school and family, and unsure what really mattered in his life. Remembering the scriptures, he chose to move forward with faith, trusting that God would help him find a way (1 Nephi 3:7), and instead of panicking, he learned to be calm and rely on God during hard moments (Exodus 14:14). He began to put God first by making better choices and using his time to help others (Jacob 2:18–19), and he committed to giving his whole effort instead of only trying halfway (Omni 1:26). One day, when he helped someone in need, he felt true happiness and understood that serving others is really serving God (Mosiah 2:17).

1 Nephi 3:7

“I will go and do the things which the Lord hath commanded…”

Exodus 14:14

“The Lord shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.”

“Before ye seek for riches, seek ye for the kingdom of God…”

Omni 1:26

“Offer your whole souls as an offering unto him…”

Mosiah 2:17

“When ye are in the service of your fellow beings ye are only in the service of your God.”
